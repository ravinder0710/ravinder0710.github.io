Production DAX Formulations

Enterprise Hospitality DAX Measures

DAX calculations engineered for target pacing, member revenue share, omnichannel marketing attribution, and stored wallet balances.

Annual Target Achievement Rate Target Pacing 
Total Sales = SUM(Fact_Sales[Gross_Amount])

Target Achievement % = 
DIVIDE(
    [Total Sales], 
    SUM(Fact_Target[Target_Amount]), 
    0
)
-- Output: 103.46% (RM20.69M vs RM20.00M Board Target)

Computes sales outperformance dynamically across year-to-date, quarter-to-date, and individual branch quotas.

Loyalty Member Sales Contribution % Loyalty Share 
Member Sales = 
CALCULATE(
    [Total Sales],
    Dim_Customer[Customer_Tier] <> "Non-Member"
)

Member Sales Contribution % = 
DIVIDE([Member Sales], [Total Sales], 0)
-- Output: 60.18% (RM12.45M Loyalty Contribution)

Measures reliance on the 8,000 registered loyalty accounts, confirming that members drive over 60% of enterprise cash flow.

Omnichannel Digital Attribution Conversion Digital Attribution 
Attributed Bookings = 
CALCULATE(
    COUNTROWS(Fact_Booking),
    Fact_Booking[Attribution_Platform_ID] <> BLANK()
)

Attributed Booking Rate = 
DIVIDE([Attributed Bookings], COUNTROWS(Fact_Booking), 0)
-- Output: 71.8% (8,774 of 12,223 Enquiries Booked)

Attributes table reservations to digital touchpoints (TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp, Search), proving multi-channel marketing ROI.

Digital Wallet Net Liquidity & Liability Wallet Float 
Net Wallet Balance = 
VAR TotalTopUps = SUM(Fact_Wallet[TopUp_Amount])
VAR TotalSpent = SUM(Fact_Wallet[Spent_Amount])
RETURN
    TotalTopUps - TotalSpent
-- Output: RM140,000 Unspent Active Stored Balance

Quantifies prepaid capital floating in member wallets, identifying dormancy risks in unspent balances across Gold and Platinum tiers.
