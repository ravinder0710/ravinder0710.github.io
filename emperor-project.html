[ problem ] · § as found

Five high-volume restaurant branches operated on disconnected legacy POS terminals and siloed reservation books. Corporate leadership had no consolidated visibility into food margins, weekend vs weekday seat yield, or VIP private dining room utilization.

Table turnover bottlenecks and unaccounted table reservation cancellations were causing estimated six-figure annual revenue leakages. Without single-direction dimensional modeling, promotional ROI across 10 digital acquisition channels could not be tied back to actual guest spend.

constraints & technical boundaries

  • Kimball 11D + 12F Architecture: Model 45K bill registers and 376K items without circular relationships or bi-directional cross-filtering performance penalties.
  • Multi-Branch Granularity: Unify 5 dining hubs with divergent dining styles (corporate banquets vs suburban family dining) into standardized operational metrics.
  • Time Intelligence & Dayparts: Split operational hours into lunch and dinner service dayparts to benchmark hourly kitchen prep and table turn rate.
  • Zero Blind Spots: Formulate actionable recovery plans for underperforming branches and dormant member wallets.

[ relational engineering ] · production dax measures

Key business metrics authored to benchmark reservation conversions and loyalty dormancy:

// 1. Booking Confirmation Conversion Rate
Booking Confirmation % = 
DIVIDE(
    CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Fact_Booking'), 'Fact_Booking'[Status] = "Confirmed"),
    COUNTROWS('Fact_Booking'),
    0
)

// 2. Net Food Margin Percentage After Wastage
Food Margin % = 
VAR TotalSales = SUM('Fact_Sales'[GrossAmount])
VAR TotalRecipeCost = SUMX('Fact_Sales', 'Fact_Sales'[Quantity] * RELATED('Dim_MenuItem'[RecipeCost]))
RETURN
DIVIDE(TotalSales - TotalRecipeCost, TotalSales, 0)

// 3. Member Wallet Liquidity & Dormancy Flag
Dormant Member Flag = 
IF(
    DATEDIFF(MAX('Fact_Sales'[OrderDate]), TODAY(), DAY) > 90,
    "Dormant (>90 Days)",
    "Active VIP"
)
