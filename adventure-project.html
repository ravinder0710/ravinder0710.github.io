Production DAX Formulations

High-Performance Retail & Inventory DAX Measures

DAX measures engineered inside Excel's xVelocity engine for fast column compression, catalog auditing, and financial ratio calculations.

Dead Stock & Unsold Catalog Audit Inventory Diagnostic 
All Products = COUNTROWS(DimProduct)

Sold Products = 
DISTINCTCOUNT(FactInternetSales[ProductKey])

Unsold Products = 
[All Products] - [Sold Products]
-- Output: 448 Unsold SKUs (73.9% Dead Stock)

Computes active versus dormant inventory lines, pinpointing working capital locked in non-moving merchandise.

Profit Margin Percentage (Safe Ratio) Profitability Ratio 
% Profit Margin = 
DIVIDE(
    [Total Profit], 
    [Total Revenue], 
    0
)
-- Output: 41.1% Enterprise Gross Margin

Utilizes safe division to evaluate operational margin across time slices, product categories, and pricing tiers.

Active Customer Base Quantification Cohort Sizing 
# Customers = 
DISTINCTCOUNT(FactInternetSales[CustomerKey])

Average Customer Spend = 
DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [# Customers], 0)
-- Output: 18,484 Active Accounts ($16,614 ARPU)

Tracks unique purchasers across demographic cohorts, establishing customer lifetime value baselines.

Demographic 50+ Profit Contribution Demographic Filter 
50+ Profit Share = 
VAR Profit50Plus = CALCULATE(
    [Total Profit],
    DimCustomer[Age_Group] = "50+"
)
RETURN
    DIVIDE(Profit50Plus, [Total Profit], 0)
-- Output: 53.56% ($67.65M Profit Share)

Isolates the purchasing power of mature sports enthusiasts, demonstrating that over half of profit stems from age 50+.
