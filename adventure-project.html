[ problem ] · § as found

Traditional flat Excel sheets crashed when loading tens of thousands of transactional rows with multiple VLOOKUP calculations. Commercial leadership had to wait hours for finance teams to consolidate multi-regional sales spreadsheets.

The enterprise required an in-memory Star Schema model that business analysts could run directly on standard commodity laptops, enabling instantaneous slicing across product categories, global regional quotas, and gross margin profit variances.

constraints & technical boundaries

  • VertiPaq / xVelocity Compression: Leverage columnar dictionary encoding and run-length compression to reduce memory footprint by over 75%.
  • Star Schema Relational Discipline: Eliminate circular dependencies, snowflake sprawl, and bi-directional filter penalties across 60,400 order rows.
  • DAX Time Intelligence: Author dynamic Year-to-Date (YTD), Year-over-Year (YoY), and Prior Year sales measures without calculated column overhead.
  • Territory Profit Variance: Benchmark gross profitability and discount elasticity across North America, Europe, and Pacific regions.

[ relational engineering ] · production dax measures

Production DAX measures authored to compute gross profit margin and Year-over-Year sales growth:

// 1. Total Retail Sales Revenue
Total Sales = 
SUMX(
    Fact_Sales,
    Fact_Sales[OrderQuantity] * Fact_Sales[UnitPrice]
)

// 2. Gross Profit Margin Percentage
Gross Profit Margin % = 
VAR TotalCost = SUMX(Fact_Sales, Fact_Sales[OrderQuantity] * Fact_Sales[TotalProductCost])
VAR Revenue = [Total Sales]
RETURN
DIVIDE(Revenue - TotalCost, Revenue, 0)

// 3. Year-over-Year (YoY) Sales Growth Rate
Sales YoY Growth % = 
VAR CurrentSales = [Total Sales]
VAR PriorYearSales = CALCULATE([Total Sales], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(Dim_Date[DateKey]))
RETURN
DIVIDE(CurrentSales - PriorYearSales, PriorYearSales, 0)
